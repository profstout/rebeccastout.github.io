The Problem I Solve

Most organizations struggling with workforce gaps face the same structural failure: industry and education operate in separate silos with misaligned incentives, timelines, and definitions of success.

  • Training programs are designed without sufficient employer input — workers graduate underprepared for actual job requirements
  • Industry invests in training partnerships that never produce pipeline volume, because education can't move at business speed
  • Internal workforce programs are built once and never maintained — they drift from operational reality within 18 months
  • Companies spend on upskilling without diagnosing the actual gap first — money goes into the wrong solution
  • Compliance and grant-funded programs are treated as overhead instead of strategic assets, leaving funding on the table

How I Work

I do not start with a program. I start with the business.

Phase 1 — Diagnose

  • Talk to workers, supervisors, and leadership — not just HR
  • Map actual skill requirements against what training delivers
  • Identify where the gap is operational, structural, or systemic
  • Audit scheduling, pricing, staffing, and delivery models

Phase 2 — Design

  • Define what "qualified" means for this role in this operation
  • Build assessments grounded in real job requirements
  • Create pipeline architecture that aligns supply with demand

Phase 3 — Build & Align

  • Stand up systems, SOPs, KPIs, and accountability structures
  • Align stakeholders around shared definitions of success
  • Build in compliance and performance tracking from the start

The result is a workforce system that functions at business speed — not academic speed.
